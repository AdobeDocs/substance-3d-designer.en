# MDL graph and Iray renderer features are missing

## End of life

MDL graphs and the Iray renderer were both <b>removed</b> in version <b>16.0.0</b> of Substance 3D Designer.

For photorealistic 3D renders, you may use our GPU Pathtracer renderer. This renderer is developed in-house and will be progressively rolled across Substance 3D applications. 

Regarding MDL support, it is being removed a part of Substance 3D moving forward with [MaterialX](https://materialx.org/) as its shading language of choice for interchangeable, widely-supported material definitions.

Version <b>15.1.3</b> is the *last* version of Designer with support for MDL graphs and the Iray renderer. Users of that feature should therefore not update to higher versions.

The documentation for these features is still available here:

* [MDL graphs](../../mdl-graphs/mdl-graphs.md)
* [Iray](../../interface/3d-view/iray/iray.md)
